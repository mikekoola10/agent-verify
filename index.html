03 — Hard boundaries

Proof of representation is not unlimited authority.

Allowed when listed

  • Asking questions and collecting written information
  • Scheduling and confirming within stated windows
  • Comparing options and returning them for owner review
  • Negotiating inside an explicit written limit

Owner approval still required

  • Purchases, payments, and binding contracts
  • Password, security, or account-access changes
  • Disclosure of credentials or identity documents
  • Anything outside the scope or past expiration
